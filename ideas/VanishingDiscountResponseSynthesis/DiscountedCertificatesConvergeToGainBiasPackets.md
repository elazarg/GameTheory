## Falsifiers and wrong turns

- Pointwise convergence of discounted values does not supply bounded bias.
- A support-changing or multichain family may have singular resolvent scales;
  an arbitrary selected limit need not satisfy one fixed architecture's domain
  conditions.
- The existence of an APS verifier is not a producer of a discounted branch.
- A concrete chattering family with no finite resolved gain--bias packet would
  refute VD2 at the proposed scope without refuting all vanishing-discount
  methods.
