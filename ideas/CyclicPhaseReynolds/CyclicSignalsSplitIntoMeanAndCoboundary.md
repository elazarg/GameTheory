# Cyclic signals split into invariant mean and coboundary

| Status | Provenance | Evidence | Reactivation condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| finite algebra `PROVED`; strategic use `OPEN`, maturity `X+I` | cyclic-phase experiments | `CyclicPhaseReynolds.lean`, phase-lifted welfare cap | actual game slack with the required factorization |

For a finite cyclic phase signal, Reynolds averaging extracts the invariant
mean and the zero-mean remainder is an exact discrete coboundary. Telescoping
the coboundary over a horizon leaves only bounded endpoints, while the mean
controls the long-run welfare term.

This is useful only if an actual game's playerwise or weighted incentive slack
is a function of the phase clock with the stated equivariance. The calculus
does not manufacture that factorization and cannot average away owner,
support, or terminal-packet labels. Without a game-facing adapter it should be
parked as independent finite algebra, not promoted as a uniform-equilibrium
route.
