# A game automorphism can transport one singleton security certificate

| Status | Provenance | Evidence | Next discriminant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| abstract transport `PROVED`; game-facing claim `OPEN`, maturity `X+I` | group-action experiment tranche | property/orbit transport Lean experiments | one nontrivial dependent state/action/history automorphism adapter |

For a player-transitive action, one representative singleton security property
plus an explicit equivariance law transports security to every player. With a
positive invariant welfare ceiling, the landed assembly theorem then yields a
uniform payoff.

An abstract player permutation is not yet a stochastic-game automorphism. It
must coherently transport states, dependent actions, transition kernels,
payoffs, initial states, histories, strategies, unilateral replacement, and
the supplied certificate. Stabilizer invariance is needed for a single-valued
selection. Until one actual game supplies these laws, this remains symmetry
reduction infrastructure rather than a producer.
