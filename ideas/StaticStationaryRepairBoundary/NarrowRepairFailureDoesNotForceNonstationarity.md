# Failure of a narrow repair grammar does not force nonstationarity

| Status | Provenance | Lean | Consumer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PROVED` fence | E37/E39 follow-up and exact calibration | source table and verifier landed | repair-ladder escalation logic |

One-owner, direct First, and owner-plus-sure-set stationary grammars can all
fail while another mixed stationary product profile is exact terminal Nash.
Therefore their universal joining/leaving obstructions do not license the
conclusion that a lasso or genuinely time-inhomogeneous profile is necessary.

Every escalation must first apply the full-rate stationary cap to arbitrary
stationary products, or explicitly state a narrower search class. This fence
does not say that stationary equilibrium always exists.
