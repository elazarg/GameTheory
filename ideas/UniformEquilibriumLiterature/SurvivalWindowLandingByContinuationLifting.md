# Survival-window landing by continuation lifting

| Status | Provenance | Consumer | Falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OPEN`, maturity `M [reported]`, P0 | Q158, repairing Simon 2007 defect №13 | `NEG-P0-2`, `MATH-P0-4`, the Theorem-3 equivalence | a global near-equilibrium whose survival jumps the window despite (K1), or a hole in the lift inequality |

## What was broken

The published Theorem 3's `(i)⇒(iii)` asserts, without derivation, that the
survival sequence of a near-equilibrium plan visits `[θρ/3, θ/3]` on its way
down — the farm's register №13, the one step in the paper its close reading
could not certify. The window's endpoint ratio is `ρ`, so it would follow from
a per-stage bound `c(p_i) ≥ ρ` at well-surviving stages; the paper's own
Lemma 5(2)(b) supplies that bound only at rows that are one-stage `ρ`-Nash
**against a `ρ`-rational continuation**. The obvious conditional-scaling repair
establishes the near-Nash half but not rationality of the continuation, whose
reach probability is destroyed by the very jump being excluded: *the jump
destroys the reach probability that would forbid the jump.*

## The repair: lift the continuation, do not certify it

`M [reported]`, verified at its two load-bearing inequalities by hand. Replace
the actual continuation coordinatewise by

> `r̂_j = max{ r_{i+1}(p)_j , χ_j }`,

which is `0`-rational **by construction**, hence `ρ`-rational for free. Then
the prescribed row is still near-Nash against it:

- **deviation side**: a deviator plays `β` at stage `i` then their own
  best-response tail — available at conditional value `≥ r̂_j − ζ` regardless
  of what `p` does after `i`, and the whole deviation conditions on reaching
  `i`, probability `S_i`, **not** `S_{i+1}`. Global equilibrium caps it, giving
  `b_j(r̂, p_i[j→β]) ≤ r_{i,j} + ε/S_i`;
- **prescribed side**: raising the continuation can only raise the row's value,
  `b_j(r̂, p_i) ≥ b_j(r_{i+1}, p_i) = r_{i,j}`.

Together: `p_i ∈ E_{ε/S_i}(r̂)`. The circle never closes because the lifted
vector's rationality costs no reach probability at all — and (K1) never
required its continuation vector to be *feasible* or generated by any plan;
each coordinate is justified by a different player's unilateral best response.
The route respects the weighted-gain warning: no endpoint-slope conversion, no
division by a hazard, the inequality proved directly for every `β`.

## The theorem, in its general form

The ambient constants `d, δ, M` were scaffolding. What holds:

- **No-large-jump lemma.** Under (K1)(b), for a global `ε`-equilibrium plan:
  `S_i ≥ ε/ρ ⟹ c(p_i) ≥ ρ`.
- **Survival-window landing.** If `ρT ≤ 1`, `ε ≤ ρT`, and `S_∞ < ρT`, then
  some stage has `S_m ∈ [ρT, T]`.

With `T = θ/3` this recovers the published window, and the published accuracy
`ε* = δθρd/(6M)` satisfies `ε*/(ρT) = δd/(2M) ≤ 1/4` — the paper's constants
carry 4× slack, and the landing already holds at `ε ≤ θρ/3`. No stronger power
of the accuracy is needed.

**One normalization caveat found in our own fence, not the paper**: the
trivial landing argument needs `θ < 3` (`S_0 = 1` must start above the
window); under the paper's intended reading `θ ∈ (0,1]` this is automatic.

## What this discharges

- **The paper's defect №13 is repaired** under the formal (K1) as stated —
  Theorem 3's `(i)⇒(iii)` no longer rests on an unsupported sentence. The
  register's other flags (№12's asserted constant, №14's loose citation) are
  untouched by this and remain open.
- **`NEG-P0-2`'s silent inheritance is discharged at `M [reported]`**: the
  orbit-side counterexample criterion can cite the packaged statement rather
  than the paper's assertion.
- The **reached-stage transfer lemma** — global `ε`-equilibrium implies
  one-stage `ε/S_i`-Nash at every reached stage, against the actual *or* any
  dominating continuation — is reusable far beyond this repair: it is the
  quitting one-shot-deviation principle with reach-probability scaling, which
  the formal development also lacks.
