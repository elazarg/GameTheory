# The block-pair table separates stationary and periodic repair

| Lifecycle | Verdict | Priority | Formalization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MINED` | `PROVED` at the audited certificate scope | `P1` stress test | generic compiler and chart algebra in Lean; concrete global certificates remain external |

For the audited four-player block-pair quitting table, exact rational
certificates exclude every stationary terminal Nash root: nonzero roots
without a sure quitter are excluded by the support certificate, sure-quitter
faces by the credibility audit, and the all-Continue root by positive solo
rewards. The same table has exact contracting period-eleven certificates, so
stationary failure is a strategy-class separation, not evidence of terminal
or uniform nonexistence.

The production library contains the table, predecessor-chart algebra, dyadic
local certificate data, the stationary gain interface, and the generic
periodic compiler. The complete stationary exclusion and the concrete
period-eleven interval/Krawczyk certificates are still external exact
certificates rather than one production theorem. They must not be cited as an
all-behavior counterexample or as an exhaustive classification of periods.

The durable lesson is that a repair ladder must include time-varying roots.
Conversely, failure of a bounded periodic search remains only a screen unless
the searched language is proved complete.
