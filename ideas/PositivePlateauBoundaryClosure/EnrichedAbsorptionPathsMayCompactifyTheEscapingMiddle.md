# Enriched absorption paths may compactify the escaping middle

| Status | Provenance | Formalization | Next discriminant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OPEN`, maturity `P+I` | Ashkenazi-Golan--Krasikov--Rainer--Solan; Q107 endpoint audit; E50/Q133 | none for the path compactification or adapter | Define the smallest marked payoff--cap absorption-path state and prove compactness plus a sound finite decoder, or exhibit information it still loses |

Absorption paths replace calendar time by accumulated absorption mass.  This
is the most natural published compactification candidate for the current
escaping-middle problem: arbitrarily long low-hazard intervals may disappear
in stage time while remaining visible through the terminal absorption measure.
It should therefore be tested before inventing a new one-point or scalar-box
compactification.

The off-the-shelf object is not yet a P0 solution.  A plateau consumer needs at
least:

- the complete terminal quitter-set measure, including the marked packet;
- the promised payoff vector and all unilateral caps, not payoff alone;
- the debt owner and its own/opponent survival clocks;
- successor/root provenance sufficient for chronological splicing; and
- a theorem turning an accepted generalized path into a finite
  accuracy-indexed behavioral profile, or into a bounded exact extension with
  cutoff-independent debt descent.

There is also a source-interface fence.  The repository audit found that the
printed sequential-perfection condition does not test a sure terminal jump in
the way needed by the repository's all-behavior terminal notion.  Until that
endpoint is repaired, the published path/equilibrium equivalence may be used
only through a proved restricted or augmented adapter.

The useful compactness scheme is consequently local and decoder-aware.  On a
compact enriched path state, it would suffice to prove at every point either an
executable repair, or an open neighborhood with some finite extension length
and positive root-debt decrement.  A finite subcover would then make the
length and decrement uniform.  This argument is valuable only after the state
space is proved compact and the local alternatives are stable; it does not
evade the Q133 length fence.

The 2026 essential-APS operator is a useful positive model for this program,
but currently covers the continuous one-randomizer-at-a-time subclass and may
have an empty payoff set.  A payoff--cap/marked-packet extension is therefore a
research direction, not an imported general producer.

**Sources.** O. Ashkenazi-Golan, I. Krasikov, C. Rainer, and E. Solan,
[*Absorption Paths and Equilibria in Quitting Games*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-022-01807-6),
and [*The APS Approach for Undiscounted Quitting Games*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-026-00982-6).
