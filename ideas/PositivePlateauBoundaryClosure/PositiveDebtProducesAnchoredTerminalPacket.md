# Positive debt produces an anchored terminal packet

| Status | Provenance | Lean | Consumer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PROVED`, maturity `M+L+A` | Q127--Q131, E50, commits `9334ab4` and `e2d5170` | positive-debt provenance and two-ended compactification modules | Boundary-holonomy source data |

A positive optimized-debt plateau selects one owner `i*` with strictly
positive singleton reward and an exact-D forward tail whose opponent-only
absorption charge is summable. Finite minimizing chains also select a nonempty
complete terminal quitter set `T*` such that the owner strictly prefers to
stay out:

\[
r_{i*}(T*)>r_{i*}(T*\cup\{i*\}).
\]

The root-to-preterminal survival factor and final action mass are distinct
positive coordinates, each bounded below at the debt scale after subsequence
selection. E50 additionally reads the same chains from the terminal end and
retains a reverse depth-one exact-D state with quantitative positive packet.

The packet's mass does not vanish in total scale, but it may escape every fixed
forward window. The owner, full action/quitter set, preterminal survival, final
atom, and finite-chain provenance must remain separate. Q129 forbids assigning
another player's local joining regret to the original owner's debt.
