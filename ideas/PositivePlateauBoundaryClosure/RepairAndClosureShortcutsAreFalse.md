| Status | Provenance | Formalization | Consumer |

The following inferences are false:

- positive zero-boundary chain debt implies no equilibrium: Q125 has a valid
  stationary repair outside that chain geometry;
- failure of owner-solo and owner-plus-sure-set grammars forces a lasso: an
  exact mixed stationary product profile can close the game;
- attainable terminal payoff/cap pairs are closed: Q132 gives vanishing
  stationary exploitability with no exact behavioral terminal equilibrium;
- local atomwise joining regret transfers dynamic-debt ownership: Q129
  separates the quantities;
- the terminal packet is measured by one survival product through the final
  root: preterminal survival and final action mass are different coordinates;
- failure of bounded periods or a finite atlas proves nonexistence: unbounded
  periods, flows, and arbitrary behavioral profiles remain.

Every proposed producer or barrier must run these regressions. Their role is
not pessimistic: they force the certificate space to retain precisely the data
needed by an executable all-behavior repair.
