# Supporting decision record — balanced systematic finite-quitting front end

**Status:** adopted coordination record; `PIPELINE.md` remains authoritative for
live `PC-*` identifiers and priorities  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** finite-quitting research, formalization, and counterexample search

## Decision

Organize the finite-quitting front by two independent research coordinates:

1. a strategic root route—stationary/projective, instant/punishment, proper
   absorption path, or nonexistence; and
2. an artifact role—producer, adapter, verifier, compiler, closure, diagnostic,
   or separator.

All positive routes meet at the all-accuracy terminal-approximation waist. The
negative semantic waist is one fixed positive terminal exploitability gap
against every behavioral profile. Existing certificate families remain in place
and retain their exact scopes; no current family is declared a universal normal
form.

The formal composition shell fixes one `QuittingSystematicSchema` before
quantifying over reward tables. A dispatcher must resolve every reward under
that same named family of positive and negative certificate languages and
compilers. It may not existentially select a new certificate language after
learning the semantic truth of each individual reward table.

Positive and negative routes are symmetric at the certificate level. Positive
certificates compile to `QuittingTerminalApproximationFamily`; concrete
negative certificates compile through `QuittingNegativeCompiler` to
`QuittingTerminalGapCertificate`. The exact fixed-gap theorem is the negative
semantic consumer, not a finite separator grammar by itself.

Adopt support-enlarging face pivots as the central cross-route producer
question: failed local data should yield a richer active face, a credible
instant-punishment boundary, a path-compatible recurrent witness, or a concrete
separator certificate. This is a research target, not a landed theorem.

The conjecture-directed flow stage is path-consistent. A global circulation
mixing incompatible recurrent SCCs is not one legal recurrent path. Search must
find a charged zero-defect circulation inside one reachable recurrent component,
or return componentwise separators for strategic decoding.

## Rationale

The repository has a strong verified back end: terminal selection and several
conditional path, cycle, APS, circulation, punishment, closure, and
nonexistence consumers. The missing theorem is a branch-complete front end from
arbitrary reward data. Scheduling by certificate family alone repeatedly lets a
new conditional compiler resemble generic progress even when no arbitrary table
can be shown to satisfy its input.

The route/role matrix preserves existing strengths while making the missing
producer or decoder explicit. The fixed-schema quantifier order removes a
formal vacuity: certificate families cannot be chosen separately for each game
inside the conjecture-facing target. Tagged semantic resolutions also preserve
the chosen route, source certificate, payoff proof, or negative gap instead of
collapsing to excluded middle.

Generic producer/adapter wrappers remain convenient composition APIs, but they
do not authenticate mathematical provenance. Route and role are justified by
the independently defined certificate language and the theorems constructing
and compiling it, not by a phantom index.

## Consequences

- Every route-facing claim records its route, role, claim level, remaining
  obligation, failure/pivot output, and next consumer.
- A compiler receives no producer credit without an explicit construction from
  game data; bounded synthesis receives no unrestricted coverage credit.
- A concrete systematic proof must define one named schema and prove a
  dispatcher for all rewards under that schema.
- The negative branch carries a concrete separator certificate and a negative
  compiler, not the semantic nonexistence proposition directly.
- Circulation-based work must identify one path-compatible recurrent component;
  cross-component convex cancellation is a diagnostic boundary, not a positive
  witness.
- The proper-absorption-path carrier/decoder remains important but is not the
  sole front. Stationary/projective preprocessing, support pivots, and concrete
  negative search remain active in parallel.
- Instant punishment is an exact boundary test and pivot destination; weaker
  passive sure-exit criteria should not be duplicated.
- A second scale, small occupation charge, compact coefficient projection, or
  relaxed finite cycle cannot be promoted to a strategic repair without the
  missing pointwise, path-realization, or decoder theorem.
- The route checker validates only structural coordination facts: JSON schema,
  enumerated values, referenced paths, markers, selected declaration names, and
  basic wiring. It does not certify mathematical classification or prose
  freshness.

## Relationship to earlier decisions

This architecture preserves `PC-001` and `PC-002`: finite quitting remains the
primary direct front and terminal approximate existence remains its positive
semantic waist. It preserves the negative lane of `PC-004`.

It refines the priority reading of `PC-003` and `PC-009`. Marked infinity-path
compactification remains an important proper-path obligation, and finite cycles
remain incomplete. But escaping-middle repair is not the unique root problem,
because the zero-pinned grammar can omit valid stationary or punishment-backed
solutions. Work on path compactification must state its pivot/decoder output and
compete by distance to the terminal waist.

## Rejected

- discard the current theorem catalog and replace it with one speculative
  universal certificate;
- existentially choose certificate languages separately for each reward table;
- return only `P ∨ ¬ P` from a supposedly proof-relevant front end;
- treat the semantic terminal gap itself as a finite negative certificate
  language;
- claim that phantom route or role indices prove mathematical provenance;
- mix incompatible recurrent components in one global circulation and postpone
  path consistency to a later stage;
- equate failure of stationary, finite-cycle, APS, bounded-controller, or one
  marked-path grammar with nonexistence; and
- continue adding back-end compilers without naming their producer input.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit this architecture if one of the following occurs:

1. a theorem proves one current route complete for arbitrary finite quitting
   games;
2. a machine-checked exhaustive pivot theorem replaces the four-lane front;
3. a concrete counterexample supplies a compiled fixed positive all-behavior
   gap;
4. a common-randomization theorem safely mixes recurrent components while
   preserving all strategic constraints; or
5. a no-go theorem proves that the route classification omits a strategically
   distinct positive profile shape.
