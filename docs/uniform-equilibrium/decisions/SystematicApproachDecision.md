# Adopt a documentation-level systematic finite-quitting approach

**Status:** active methodological decision  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** finite-quitting research and formalization

## Decision

Organize conjecture-directed work by:

1. broad root route — stationary/projective, instant/punishment, proper
   absorption path, or nonexistence;
2. artifact role — producer, adapter, verifier/compiler, closure, diagnostic,
   or separator; and
3. claim level — supplied-object verification, restricted synthesis, or
   arbitrary-table coverage.

Use [`../SystematicApproach.md`](../SystematicApproach.md) as stable guidance and
[`../ConjectureDirectedSpine.md`](../ConjectureDirectedSpine.md) as the proposed
mathematical dependency program. `PIPELINE.md` remains the only live priority
and project-control source.

Do **not** encode these research classifications as generic proof-relevant Lean
wrappers or ask CI to authenticate mathematical provenance. Lean should contain
substantive statements and useful composition theorems. Review and source
maintenance should enforce the organizational distinctions.

## Mathematical correction

The flow stage of the conjecture-directed spine is componentwise. A global
circulation may cancel signed defects across recurrent SCCs that no single path
can visit recurrently. Therefore the positive alternative must choose one
reachable recurrent SCC, and the negative alternative may require one dual
separator per reachable component rather than one global separator.

This path-consistency condition is load-bearing and is recorded explicitly in
the spine, together with the two-disconnected-loop regression.

## Rationale

The repository's main weakness is not incorrect execution of a generic front-end
API. It is the attempted object: research provenance, route authenticity, and
producer credit are mathematical and methodological claims that a generic
function-valued record cannot establish.

The useful parts are:

- the existing producer/compiler/diagnostic distinction;
- one coherent conjecture-directed dependency spine;
- preservation of exact failure and pivot payloads;
- an unrestricted negative lane; and
- the substantive fixed-gap theorem showing that terminal exploitability is the
  exact negative semantic target.

Removing the generic wrappers and route CI avoids giving formal weight to labels
that remain conventional, while retaining the mathematics and research
coordination they were meant to serve.

## Consequences

- New Lean is added only for actual mathematical content or composition that a
  downstream proof uses.
- A compiler does not receive producer credit without a theorem constructing its
  input from stated game data.
- Failed route tests should return the concrete support, inequality, recurrent
  component, target mismatch, or separator datum needed by another route.
- A global convex occupation is not treated as path-realizable until one
  recurrent-component and single-path realization theorem is proved.
- Failure of a restricted grammar is not called nonexistence until it reaches a
  fixed positive all-behavior terminal exploitability gap.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit the organizational taxonomy if a theorem proves one route complete, a
counterexample establishes the fixed positive gap, or a new strategic profile
shape is shown not to fit any current route. Revisit the SCC condition only if a
common-randomization theorem proves that mixing whole recurrent components is a
legal strategic implementation preserving all incentive constraints.
