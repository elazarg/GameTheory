# Adopt a documentation-level systematic finite-quitting approach

**Status:** active methodological decision  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Scope:** finite-quitting research and formalization

## Decision

Organize conjecture-directed work by:

1. broad root route—stationary/projective, instant/punishment, proper
   absorption path, or nonexistence;
2. artifact role—producer, adapter, verifier/compiler, closure, diagnostic,
   or separator; and
3. claim level—supplied-object verification, restricted synthesis, or
   arbitrary-table coverage.

Use [`../SystematicApproach.md`](../SystematicApproach.md) as stable guidance and
[`../ConjectureDirectedSpine.md`](../ConjectureDirectedSpine.md) as the proposed
mathematical dependency program. `PIPELINE.md` remains the only live priority
and project-control source.

Do **not** encode these research classifications as a generic proof-relevant
Lean front end or ask CI to authenticate mathematical provenance. Lean should
contain substantive statements and useful composition theorems. Review and
source maintenance should enforce organizational distinctions that have no
honest generic type-level witness.

## Why the generic formal front end was rejected

The rejected design failed for precise reasons, not merely because its API was
awkward.

1. **Per-reward certificate languages can hide the conclusion.** If certificate
   types are chosen after a reward table, a positive language can be defined as
   a subtype carrying `QuittingTerminalApproximationFamily reward`. Classical
   excluded middle then supplies either that hidden positive certificate or the
   exact negative gap. Existence of such a front-end object proves no producer
   theorem.
2. **A phantom route index does not authenticate a route.** A compiler record
   whose only field is a function to the semantic waist can be rewrapped under
   another route index. Likewise, generic producer and adapter records are both
   just function wrappers; the named mathematics, not the wrapper, establishes
   provenance.
3. **Returning `P ∨ ¬ P` erases the evidence.** Such a semantic theorem is
   available classically without any front end and forgets the route,
   certificate, compiler, payoff, and negative gap.
4. **The fixed terminal gap is a semantic waist, not a finite separator
   grammar.** A balanced negative route would still need a concrete certificate
   language and a theorem compiling it to the all-behavior gap.

Fixing only the quantifier order would remove the first easy construction but
would not let Lean decide whether a named reward-indexed certificate family is
circular. The honest boundary is therefore documentation-level taxonomy plus
specific mathematical certificate types and compilers as they are actually
proved.

## Mathematical correction

The flow stage of the conjecture-directed spine is componentwise. A global
circulation may cancel signed defects across recurrent SCCs that no single path
can visit recurrently. Therefore the positive alternative must choose one
reachable recurrent SCC, and the negative alternative may require one dual
separator per reachable component rather than one global separator.

This path-consistency condition is load-bearing and is recorded explicitly in
the spine, together with the two-disconnected-loop regression. Even a
component circulation remains only occupation data: connectors between cycles,
signed discrepancy, and physical realization are separate Stage-E costs.

## Rationale

The repository's main weakness is not incorrect execution of a generic
front-end API. It is the attempted object: research provenance, route
authenticity, and producer credit are mathematical and methodological claims
that a generic function-valued record cannot establish.

The useful parts are:

- the existing producer/compiler/diagnostic distinction;
- one coherent conjecture-directed dependency spine;
- preservation of exact failure and pivot payloads;
- an unrestricted negative lane; and
- the substantive fixed-gap theorem showing that terminal exploitability is the
  exact negative semantic target.

Removing the generic wrappers and route CI avoids giving formal weight to
labels that remain conventional, while retaining the mathematics and research
coordination they were meant to serve.

## Consequences

- New Lean is added only for actual mathematical content or composition that a
  downstream proof uses.
- A compiler does not receive producer credit without a theorem constructing its
  input from stated game data.
- Failed route tests should return the concrete support, inequality, recurrent
  component, target mismatch, or separator datum needed by another route.
- A global convex occupation is not treated as path-realizable until one
  recurrent-component and single-path realization theorem is proved.
- Failure of a restricted grammar is not called nonexistence until it reaches a
  fixed positive all-behavior terminal exploitability gap.
- Future concrete certificate types may use route-specific structures, but no
  generic wrapper is advertised as proving their provenance.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit the organizational taxonomy if a theorem proves one route complete, a
counterexample establishes the fixed positive gap, or a new strategic profile
shape is shown not to fit any current route. Revisit the SCC condition only if a
common-randomization theorem proves that mixing whole recurrent components is a
legal strategic implementation preserving all incentive constraints.
