\begin{abstract}
This is a research atlas rather than a publication claiming a new general
existence theorem.  It is written for a mathematically capable project owner,
adjacent researchers, and future proof agents.  Its compact evidence tags
separate formal results, experiments, audited mathematics, hypotheses, open
problems, and fenced-off proof patterns.

The central open statement is that every finite stochastic game has a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  The repository has a semantic verification waist,
discounted and analytic source machinery, public-response composition,
several full special cases, and a detailed finite-quitting reduction.  The
quitting front carries, in production Lean: the
exact punishment value as a stationary stopping value (both inequalities, all
history-dependent plans); an exact characterization of terminal sure-exit
sets; a total two-blocker branch gate; an exact characterization of the
one-stage collision-repair mechanism priced against the true punishment
value; and an abstract duality identifying bounded orbit budgets with bounded
potentials, calibrated against the repository's own one-stage operator.  It
additionally contains fixed-skeleton reward perturbation and
target-free payoff-existence closure, a support-witness compiler from
divergent paths or finite absorbing cycles, and an adaptive-mesh nonperiodic
compiler proving uniform payoffs throughout the compact terminal-free
functional unique-live essential-APS stratum.  It now also contains a genuine
conditional producer class: a supplied bounded multi-owner face circulation
with phase ratios uniformly below one and floor above formal punishment values
compiles, already from its finite charge-target quantifiers, to an existential
uniform payoff by compact charged return and one-seam closing;
the scaled cyclic and repaired four-player stress weights are concrete cases.
Finite phase-occupation LP duality is production only conditional on an
existing occupation and is not a strategic producer.
Audited mathematics adds a no-resurrection theorem (vanishing phase
occupation cannot repair a pointwise-invalid phase), a support-enlarged
two-owner mechanism, and an exactly characterized local residue for
three-coordinate weights.  Production Lean proves that the residue is nonempty
and that its parametric family is absorbed by an omitted exact
double-sure-quitter/mixer root.  The earlier holonomy layer stands: exact
associative boundary holonomy, fixed-cutoff compact closed lifts, and the
literal-length fence.  Matching-order analytic germs yield normalized
cemetery/singleton packets; signed survival-weighted monodromy is the exact
periodic correction coordinate, and arbitrarily charged finite forward packets
close through one-seam projective lassos.  Punishment-completed
cycles strictly extend the former nonnegative-solo cycle class.  The combined
quitting counterexample normal form is also exact: positive all-behavior
terminal gap plus finite canonical floor-prefix charge capacity.  It forces
summable absorption, finite toggle/stationary and packet-support screens, and a
canonical minimal player set of cardinality at least four; two- and
three-player quitting games are unconditional positive cases.  Periodic
behavioral replies now reduce exactly to a finite stop/refusal maximum, while
cap-seeded splicing isolates the remaining suffix co-realization premise.
Conversely,
an explicit two-player zero-sum game refutes the universal truncated-ledger
producer while retaining an exact all-Continue uniform equilibrium.  Exactly
two intentional open declarations remain; every remaining arrow is tagged
where it appears.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Uniform equilibrium, with its quantifiers visible}

Let \(I\) be a finite player set, \(S\) a finite state space, and
\(A_i(s)\) finite nonempty action sets.  A finite stochastic game has bounded
stage rewards \(g_i(s,a)\) and transition law \(q(\,\cdot\mid s,a)\).
Histories, states, and realized action profiles are public; players have
perfect recall and private independent randomization.  The base model grants
no free public correlation device.

For a behavior profile \(\sigma\), write
\[
 \gamma_N^i(s,\sigma)
 =\EE_{s,\sigma}\left[\frac1N\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}g_i(s_t,a_t)\right].
\]
A vector \(v\in\RR^I\) is a uniform-equilibrium payoff from \(s\) when
\[
 \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\sigma\ \exists N_0\
 \forall N\geq N_0\ \forall i\in I\ \forall\alpha_i,\qquad
 |\gamma_N^i(s,\sigma)-v_i|\leq\varepsilon,\qquad
 \gamma_N^i(s,\alpha_i,\sigma_{-i})\leq v_i+\varepsilon .
\tag{UE}
\]
The profile may depend on the accuracy, but not on the eventual horizon.
The deviator may be a history-dependent behavior strategy.  Thus a
horizon-by-horizon Nash profile, a limiting-average payoff, or a discounted
equilibrium does not by itself solve (UE).

\subsection{Program map}

\[
\boxed{\begin{array}{c}
\text{finite game}\\
\downarrow\quad\text{discounted or finite-chain source data}\\
\text{implementable full payoff target and legal continuation}\\
\downarrow\quad\text{delivery, all unilateral caps, and rebasing}\\
\text{adaptive certificate / deviation-cap constructor}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{uniform-equilibrium payoff.}
\end{array}}
\tag{1}
\]

The final verification arrow has an \LTag consumer.  The generic difficulty is
the middle.  A construction must retain the whole target vector, make every
public entry actually used by its compiler credible on the prescribed path and
on the declared one-deviator response domain, and exhibit a well-founded
decrease or a fully discharged account.  This is weaker than demanding
subgame perfection at every unreachable node, but stronger than checking only
the initial history.  A scalar limit or an attractive Bellman equality cannot
carry those obligations automatically.

An undiscounted equilibrium can be a limit along changing approximate
profiles, while a uniform payoff requires one profile for all sufficiently
large horizons at each accuracy.  A conversion therefore needs a named
argument.  Positive recursive games obtain one from monotonic finite
averages; finite quitting games use a different, formalized
terminal-to-uniform bridge.

\section{Production foundation: the two waists}

\subsection{Semantic cap constructor}

\Status{\Prod} The theorem
\texttt{hasUniformDeviationCapConstructor\_iff} says that a quantitative
delivery-and-deviation-cap constructor is exactly (UE).  A future proof may
use another construction language, but it must ultimately expose those
quantifiers.

\subsection{Fixed-skeleton reward closure}

\Status{\Prod} Replacing only the stage-payoff table preserves every finite
history law.  If the two reward tables differ uniformly by at most \(\rho\),
then every prescribed or unilaterally deviating finite-horizon payoff differs
by at most \(\rho\); an \(\varepsilon\)-Nash profile for the nearby table is
therefore an \((\varepsilon+2\rho)\)-Nash profile for the original table with
the same horizon threshold.

The target-free closure theorem assumes only that uniformly convergent nearby
tables each admit some uniform-equilibrium payoff.  Finiteness bounds those
targets in one payoff cube, a convergent payoff-vector subsequence is selected,
and the nearby profiles are transferred directly.  No strategy is passed to a
limit and no common memory bound is assumed.  Consequently, proving existence
on a dense class of reward tables proves existence for every table on that
fixed skeleton.  Establishing density of a useful solved class is a separate
game-specific obligation; transition-kernel perturbations are not covered.

\subsection{Adaptive potential verification}

\Status{\Prod} \texttt{AdaptiveCertificate.lean} verifies expectation-level
adaptive potential certificates.  At accuracy \(\delta\), a public profile is
equipped, playerwise, with lower and upper on-path potentials and a deviation
upper potential.  Their initial values are close to \(v\); on-path processes
have the right expected sub/supermartingale directions; and the deviation
bound applies to every unilateral behavior.

If \(L_i,U_i,D_i\) denote the potentials, the proof has the telescoping
shape
\[
\EE\sum_{t<N}(g_i(t)-v_i)
\quad\hbox{between}\quad
\EE[L_i(N)-L_i(0)]-o(N)
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\EE[U_i(N)-U_i(0)]+o(N),
\tag{2}
\]
with the same upper estimate using \(D_i\) after a unilateral replacement.
Division by \(N\) gives (UE), uniformly in the deviator.  Branch cancellation
is allowed; a construction need not have a pathwise nonnegative account, but
it must compile to this expectation-level control or directly to the semantic
cap constructor.

\subsection{Supplied public response architectures}

\Status{\Prod} A separate verification route handles a supplied finite public
Markov response architecture.  It separates the target and occupation data:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{(T0)} & v=P^0v\quad\text{on the prescribed delivery domain},\\
\text{(Ti)} & v_i\geq P_i^a v_i\quad\text{on player \(i\)'s owner arena},\\
\text{(N)} & \text{every invariant owner-local occupation has nonpositive
reward charge},\\
\text{(P)} & \text{the prescribed delivery law matches the target}.
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
Gain--bias/Poisson inequalities convert these conditions to bounded
potentials, a common \(O(1/N)\) delivery bound, unilateral caps, and
rebasing.  The domain split is load-bearing: the prescribed delivery union is
not the union of every player's deviation arena.

The fixed-class converse is also available.  All-start history semantics on
these exact domains imply target, occupation, and bias packets.
This characterizes a supplied architecture; it does not synthesize an
architecture for an arbitrary game or show that finite public architectures
cover all uniform strategies.

\subsection{Composition and concrete controllers}

\Status{\Prod} Finite public trees have backward Nash and fixed-depth adaptive
splicing.  Public stopping modules handle bounded causal stopping, stopped
child laws, prefix accounting, and explicit amortization horizons.  They make
real child systems composable; they do not create a missing child or a free
public signal.

\Status{\Prod} The Flesch--Thuijsman--Vrieze three-player quitting example is
implemented as a ten-configuration controller with a three-phase live clock
and seven absorbing children.  It satisfies the credibility conditions and
delivers \((1,2,1)\).  Under the repository's convention the all-start
finite-horizon coordinate errors are
\[
 16/(7N),\qquad22/(7N),\qquad18/(7N).
\tag{4}
\]
The uniform constant is \(22/(7N)\); the smaller value sometimes quoted uses
a different convention for the quitting stage.  This is an exact test of the
verifier for supplied data, not a generic controller producer.

\section{Discounted analytic source data}

\subsection{The analytic germ and endpoint atlas}

For normalized discount \(\lambda\), a stationary discounted equilibrium has
\[
 V_i^\lambda(s)=\EE_{s,\sigma^\lambda}
 \left[\sum_{t\geq0}\lambda(1-\lambda)^t g_i(s_t,a_t)\right].
\tag{5}
\]
\Status{\Prod} \texttt{AnalyticBellmanExistence.lean} proves that every finite
game has, after finite reparameterization, a coupled real-analytic Bellman
germ
\[
 t\longmapsto(\lambda(t),\sigma(t),V(t)),\qquad\lambda(t)\downarrow0.
\tag{6}
\]
On a punctured interval the finite families of supports, equality patterns,
and signs stabilize.  \texttt{AnalyticEndpointAtlas.lean} and
\texttt{AnalyticEndpointExistence.lean} then classify a first unprocessed
response into a finite typed leaf family carrying player, support, analytic
order, and occupation information.

This is source organization, not strategic closure.  A nonsemantic leaf may
ask for a response, target transport, entry proof, or credibility witness.
Such a field must remain an explicit hypothesis; it is not a generic proof
hidden inside an interface.

\subsection{Endpoint is not target}

\Status{\Prod} Sorin's absorbing example permanently blocks the most tempting
shortcut.  The relevant discounted equilibrium payoff is
\[
 z^\lambda=(1/2,\,2/3),
\]
but the repository proves that this endpoint is not a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  The proof combines security, live-tail
accounting, and occupation vanishing.  The converse characterization of the
whole uniform segment from Sorin's paper is not formalized \cite{Sorin1986}.
Thus
\[
 \text{discounted analytic source}\ \not\Rightarrow\
 \text{preserve its endpoint as a uniform root target}.
\tag{7}
\]
One may need both favorable-germ selection and a different, implementable
target selection.

\subsection{Analytic infrastructure}

\Status{\Prod} The library also contains sign-stable Bellman rows,
neutral-action drift alternatives, harmonic directions, support-face and
deflation tools, player-owned and neutral occupation/circulation alternatives,
public separator compression, causal stopping and target-transport
interfaces, and rank-aware public recurrent-class machinery.
\Status{\Audit} The associated durable mathematics includes sharp neutral

The repeated obstacle is whole-target custody.  A scalar circulation or

\subsection{Matching projective packets}

\Status{\Prod} In the matching-order regime, let the discount and quitting
rates have leading data
\[
  \lambda(t)=t^m,\qquad
  q_i(t)=a_i t^m+o(t^m),\qquad a_i\geq0.
\]
The exact first-event identities imply that, after normalization by
\(1+\sum_i a_i\), the cemetery mass tends to
\(1/(1+\sum_i a_i)\), singleton \(i\) has mass
\(a_i/(1+\sum_i a_i)\), and all nonsingleton mass vanishes.  The Bellman
identity and endpoint complementarity then give
\[
 V_k(0)=\sum_i\frac{a_i}{1+\sum_j a_j}\,r_k(\{i\}),
 \qquad r_i(\{i\})\leq V_i(0),
\]
with equality in the second relation whenever \(a_i>0\).  Thus an analytic
matching germ produces the normalized singleton projective-LCP packet needed
by the finite projective layer.  This passage explicitly restricts to the
punctured physical slice \(0<t<\min\{g.\mathrm{radius},1\}\), hence
\(0<\lambda(t)\leq1\); an algebraic Bellman
sign variety alone does not supply that domain.

\Status{\Refuted} Packet extraction does not certify the endpoint as an
undiscounted strategic target.  Consider the two-player quitting table
\[
 r(\{1\})=(1,2),\qquad
 r(\{2\})=(2,1),\qquad
 r(\{1,2\})=(2,2).
\]
At discount factor \(1-t\), both players quitting with probability
\(t/(1-t)\) is an exact analytic stationary Bellman branch with live value
\((1,1)\).  It has matching order and normalized first-event weights
\(z_0=z_1=z_2=1/3\).  Yet if a terminal \(\varepsilon\)-Nash payoff is
coordinatewise \(\delta\)-close to \((1,1)\), the late deterministic-quit
deviation and opponent-survival product inequality give
\[
  1-\delta \leq 4(\delta+\varepsilon).
\]
Thus equal errors satisfy \(\eta\geq1/9\), and \((1,1)\) is not a uniform
equilibrium payoff.  The same game has exact uniform payoffs \((1,2)\) and
\((2,1)\) from the two singleton sure-exit profiles.  A positive cemetery
coordinate is therefore an affine accounting anchor, not an executable
continuation contract.  Strategic production must first accept the packet
target with such a contract or reject it and provide a certified retarget.

\Status{\Prod} Zero-anchor and affine-anchor singleton LCP algebra and exact
signed-monodromy correction turn a supplied rotation-uniform projective return
into an exact support-rational absorbing cycle, hence into a uniform-equilibrium
payoff.  If an upstream theorem instead supplies arbitrarily charged exact
finite forward packets in one compact carrier, finite charged return closes a
one-seam lasso automatically.  Every finite quitting game now supplies exact
product-root predecessor prefixes above the behavioral punishment floor.  The
compiler permits the prefix to depend on the charge target.  Therefore either
these finite prefixes produce a uniform payoff, or one common finite charge
bound controls every exact prefix in the canonical box with a
punishment-rational anchor.  This bypasses selection of one charge-complete
infinite orbit; the previously selected predecessor orbit is only a special
case.  On the exact-predecessor relation reachable from the punishment-floor
anchor, the common bound becomes a finite path budget.  Its canonical
budget-to-go is a bounded Bellman potential which decreases by at least the
literal absorption mass of every predecessor row.  This certificate does not
itself produce a strategic tail: unlike a marked calibrated cylinder, it carries
no packet, debt, obstacle graph, or realization map.  The remaining
accepted-target obligations include chart coverage and arc lifting, semantic
decoding of Farkas alternatives, and a consumer for this game-intrinsic
bounded-potential branch.  A general accept-or-retarget dispatcher is an
additional upstream obligation.  Compact recurrence or repeated labels alone
still do not imply the required return estimate.

\section{Finite quitting games: exhaustive reduction to one residual}

\subsection{Model and terminal semantics}

A finite quitting game has one live state.  At every live date each player
chooses Continue or Quit; the first nonempty quitter set \(J\subseteq I\)
absorbs with reward vector \(r(J)\), while Never has payoff zero.  For a

\Status{\Prod} The essential-APS route supplies a different, conditional

\subsection{Positive routes outside the quitting producer}

\paragraph{Analytic leaf closure or arc improvement.}
The endpoint atlas can be attacked leaf by leaf, or a theorem can select a
more useful analytic germ.  Either approach still must select an implementable
root target and compile legal public continuations.  Sorin prevents treating
germ selection alone as sufficient.

\paragraph{Fixed architecture synthesis.}
The gain--bias characterization in (3) is a clear synthesis target.  A
positive theorem would produce a finite controller, declare its delivery union
and owner arenas, prove target transport and neutral occupation, then invoke
the existing verifier.  The remaining questions are why a suitable finite
architecture exists, how large it must be, and whether general strategies
require hidden or unbounded memory.

\paragraph{Vanishing-discount response synthesis.}
\Status{\Open} A controlled analytic family of discounted
APS/self-generating certificates might have a gain--bias limit
\[
 V^\lambda\approx g+\lambda^{-1}h,\qquad g+h=r+Ph.
\tag{24}
\]
The consumer is already available.  Pointwise value convergence does not
supply bounded bias: changing supports, multichain classes, and singular
resolvent scales can invalidate one fixed architecture.  A positive result
needs stable domains/support or a sound resolved limit; a chattering example
would delimit this route only.

\paragraph{$Q$-matrix preprocessing and the three-player oracle.}
\Status{\Audit} Solan--Solan prove that if their derived quitting matrix is not
a $Q$-matrix, ordinary uniform approximate equilibria exist
\cite{SolanSolan2020}.  Once the matrix normalization and payoff convention are
adapted exactly, the safe preprocessing order is
\[
 R(\Gamma)\notin Q\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{discharge by the published result},
 \qquad
 R(\Gamma)\in Q\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{run the P0 machinery}.
\tag{25}
\]
It is not known that a positive optimized-debt plateau forces
$R(\Gamma)\in Q$.  A machine-checked two-player regression already shows that
positive debt in the selected zero-boundary grammar can coexist with an
elementary stationary equilibrium whose payoff lies outside that chain
geometry.  Thus a plateau-to-$Q$ implication would be a new compatibility
theorem, not a corollary of external existence.

Solan's three-player absorbing theorem is the nearest solved oracle
\cite{Solan1999}.  The high-value mining target is not a wholesale port of its
proof, but a scoped theorem that any three-player positive-debt plateau exposes
one of P0-C/P0-D's corrected repair or surgery outputs.  This would test the
proposed grammar where simultaneous packets and player transitions first
become nontrivial.

\paragraph{Absorption-path APS.}
The essential-APS operator of \cite{AshkenaziGolanEtAl2026} suggests invariant
sets and successor rules for continuous absorption paths on which at most one
player randomizes at a mass time.  Its characterized payoff subset may be
empty.  It should be mined for the structure of $V$ and $\mathcal O$, not
reported as coverage of the positive plateau.  The repository formalizes both
the finite singleton-flow interface and a conditional infinite positive
stratum.  Consecutive proper arcs force the Flesch successor relation;
executable one-continuation segments embed into the larger full-convex-hull
operator; and the restricted operator has a greatest algebraic fixed family.
On a compact functional unique-live terminal-free component with finite-window
face avoidance, coherent execution, qualitative deleted-player survival decay,
and adaptive-mesh compilation prove that every initial component value
is a uniform payoff.  The zero-mass self-loop regression and the structural
component hypotheses prevent reading this as a converse realization or
arbitrary-game coverage theorem.

\paragraph{Endogenous correlation.}
The correlated-existence theorem points to a possible mechanism but also a
boundary.  An autonomous device uses private current recommendations and
delayed disclosure; it is not merely a public random bit.  Public-coin and
correlation-separator modules test legal pieces of this question, including a
target-specific absorbing lift that independent randomization cannot realize.
A compiler must reproduce the needed information and obedience structure with
legal play and sublinear cost.

\subsection{What a negative route would have to prove}

A genuine counterexample needs a finite game and \(\eta>0\) such that every
behavior profile has a player and unilateral behavior deviation with the
required positive gain at the relevant long horizon.  Failures of stationary,
periodic, finite-memory, owner-solo, or selected-chain grammars do not meet
this burden.

For finite quitting games this burden now has an exact combined normal form.
Nonexistence is equivalent to a positive all-behavior terminal gap \(\eta\)
together with finiteness of the canonical absorption-charge capacity \(C^*\)
of all boxed exact punishment-floor prefixes.  Dynamic debt is derived:
\[
 0<\eta\leq \inf_N D_N\leq D_N
 \leq \max_i\max\{0,r_i(\{i\})\}\leq M.
\]
The optimized exact-$D$ tail is summably absorbing and converges toward an
all-Continue positive-debt self-loop annotation.  Dynamic debt is an exact
survival-conservation coordinate:
\[
 d_s(i)=\beta_{s,\infty}D_i+
 \sum_{k\geq0}\beta_{s,k}p_{s+k}(i)d_{s+k}(i).
\]
For the selected owner, eventual deleted survival is exactly the endpoint
debt ratio and the remaining opponent clock is at most
\(\log(K_i/\eta)\).  Every positive-debt coordinate is above its punishment
floor at every finite date.  If a coordinate lies below
its punishment value by \(\delta>0\) at date \(s\), the division-free estimate
\[
 \delta\sum_{t\geq s}\text{opponent-clock}_i(t)\leq \chi_i+M
\]
holds.  The canonical capacity is a bounded potential on the entire boxed
floor-admissible predecessor relation, not only one selected orbit.

The regime is empty below four players.  Any hypothetical finite
counterexample can therefore be reindexed to a cardinal-minimal table on
\(\operatorname{Fin}(n)\), \(n\geq4\), whose nonempty proper player
restrictions are solved as restricted games.  This does not control excluded
players' joining deviations.  Exact toggle and stationary-cap ceilings,
supported packet atoms, and closed weak-preference walks provide finite search
filters, but packet support is not itself a chronological Bellman orbit.  The
closed normalized packet space has a table-dependent uniform positive defect:
every packet contains an active atom with refusal advantage bounded below by
one common constant.

Against periodically repeated opponents, the complete behavioral best reply is
exactly the maximum of finitely many first-pass stop values and the
refusal/Never value; no contraction premise is needed.  The discrepancy between
a convergent Bellman annotation and the actual terminal payoff is exactly the
limiting joint survival times the limiting boundary annotation, with explicit
tail-charge moduli.  In particular, favorable signed drift can overdeliver
while the restart is behaviorally unexploitable.  On the optimized tail,
honest payoff from late starts tends to zero while one prescribed coordinate
tends to a limit at least \(\eta\): the obstruction is a positive phantom
plateau on the Never boundary.  The canonical length-\(n+1\) restarted tail
windows are blocked from the first window, and an infinite subfamily has one
fixed obstructing player and one fixed refusal-versus-phase branch.  This does
not attach such a suffix to an arbitrary earlier exact-$D$ prefix.  A bounded rational
four-player singleton table has a positive compact escape separation for every
owner distribution, so singleton complementarity cannot be assumed; this is a
grammar regression, not a quitting-game counterexample.

For cap-seeded exact prefixes, cap-minus-realized-value transports exactly by
joint survival.  If one suffix co-realizes the terminal cap as its complete
behavioral deviation envelope, a terminal gap implies both a survival lower
bound and a logarithmic upper bound on prefix absorption charge.  The
co-realization premise is the remaining strategic seam.  An exact two-player
regression shows that positive augmented cap data may coexist with a unique
all-Continue cap root of zero absorption in a solved game.
Closed projective passage nevertheless places every optimized-tail augmented
cap in the global floor-admissible carrier, and its limiting cap is a literal
zero-charge all-Continue self-loop there.  The finite diagonal seam prevents
promoting the chronological exact-$D$ tail itself to a path in that relation.

For an actual stationary product profile \(x\), let \(u_i(x)\) be terminal
payoff and \(c_i(x)\) its exact full-rate behavior cap.  The honest stationary
filter is
\[
 g_{\mathrm{stat}}(x)=\max_i\max\{0,c_i(x)-u_i(x)\},\qquad
 \delta_{\mathrm{stat}}=\inf_x g_{\mathrm{stat}}(x).
\tag{26}
\]
\Status{\Audit} If \(\delta_{\mathrm{stat}}=0\), actual stationary terminal
approximate equilibria exist at every accuracy; if it is positive, only the
stationary class is excluded.  A graph-closure zero is an escape family, not
an attained profile.

The two-player table
\[
 r(\{1\})=(1,-1),\quad r(\{2\})=(-1,-1),\quad r(\{1,2\})=(-2,0)
\tag{27}
\]
has stationary exploitability tending to zero but no exact behavioral terminal
Nash equilibrium.  It refutes closed-attainable-tail and compact-zero-implies-
root arguments, not finite-quitting existence.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Test density before compactifying strategies.}  Compute the
closure of the payoff tables covered by the proved stationary, cyclic,
support-enlarged, and path mechanisms.  If it is dense, fixed-skeleton payoff
closure proves existence everywhere without a strategy limit.  If its
complement has an open cell, select a rational interior table for obstruction
search.
\item \textbf{Consume the combined counterexample regime.}  Starting from the
forced supported singleton packet and the summably absorbing positive-debt
tail, prove a strategically legal alternative: complementary mixture,
support-enlarged positive-charge exact return, or an unblocked periodic
restart.  The three branches are already consumed respectively by circulation,
finite capacity, and the terminal gap; the missing theorem must use exact
product-root dynamics and nonsingleton collision rewards.
\item \textbf{Build a path-consistent support atlas.}  The local residue is
nonempty but has an exact omitted-cell absorber, so the next finite grammar
must enlarge support instead of escalating automatically to long cycles.
Construct a finite graph of strategically legal local transitions whose every
boundary is typed as terminal, proper subgame, support enlargement, target
rejection, or physical-arc failure.
\item \textbf{Prove quantitative subgame gluing.}  If insider absorption atoms
are at most \(\delta\) and every outsider suffix value is within \(\eta_i\)
below its solo option, the outsider's quit gain is at most
\(\eta_i+2M\delta\).  Lift this deterministic quit-time estimate through the
landed stopping-law mixture theorem; on failure retain the entering outsider
or marked profitable joining coalition.
\item \textbf{Complete the projective producer.}  Build and cover the resolved
Bellman charts, place an explicit accept-or-certified-retarget gate before
target-preserving realization, lift accepted feasible lexicographic tangents
to physical arcs, and on each reachable recurrent component produce a charged
zero-defect flow or a strategically decoded separator.  A feasible legal word
should feed the signed compiler through cyclic Abel discrepancy; an exact
finite-forward producer may instead use the landed finite charged-closing
adapter.  Matching analytic packet extraction, target mismatch, and both
downstream compilers are already closed.
\item \textbf{Close cap realization without axioms.}  The finite cap-seeded
splice and logarithmic charge estimate are exact.  Construct one admissible
suffix that co-realizes its prescribed terminal payoff and complete behavioral

\section{Acceptance test and conclusion}

Before accepting a generic closure, ask:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Which source branch is selected, and why is the complete root target
implementable rather than merely a limit?
\item What actual behavior profile or public architecture exists at each
accuracy?
\item Why are all unilateral history-dependent deviations capped?
\item Where is every target coordinate transported through public branching
and stopping?
\item For a quitting boundary object, which missing mass is a finite exit port
and which is genuine Never?  Where are the conditional marked packet and the
full stopping obstacle retained?
\item How is a sure terminal jump tested against the off-path continuation
created when a prescribed quitter Continues?
\item Why is entry/rebasing legal after every invoked public history?
\item What decreases well-foundedly, or which realized account is discharged?
Is that decrease uniform enough to contradict a plateau?
\item Which existing semantic verifier consumes the object without a circular
field that asks for uniform equilibrium again?
\item Does it survive the permanent regressions: Sorin target mismatch, Big
Match adaptivity, split-domain failure, behavioral nonattainment,
owner-transfer failure, and static sure-set incompleteness?
\end{enumerate}

The repository is no longer pursuing a vague claim that discounted equilibria
should converge.  It has a checked semantic waist and several independently
useful theorem families.  The finite-quitting route has an exhaustive
zero-versus-positive-debt split, an exhaustive owner-clock split, exact
finite-middle holonomy with fixed-cutoff closure and a precise literal-length
obstruction, an exact punishment value, an exactly characterized one-stage
terminal mechanism layer, matching analytic projective packets, and
punishment-completed exact cycles.

The semantic layer also has compact diagnostic consequences: profile-uniform
vanishing finite-average payoff gaps preserve each exact target, with bounded
expected-potential shaping as an endpoint-telescope instance.  Conversely,
arbitrarily thin eventual prescribed/deviation intervals and bounded
excess-work certificates characterize the relevant uniform-payoff statements;
these are reverse tests, not generic producers.  Rare-transition examples
separately rule out unrestricted continuity under transition-kernel limits.

Two boundaries constrain what the chain machinery can be asked to do.  Uniform
middle-length tightness is false, and no bounded modification of a minimizer
lowers its optimized debt by a cutoff-independent amount.  Both are settled by
explicit two-player weights.  Those same weights show that a positive optimized
debt need not say anything about the game: pinning the terminal continuation to
zero forces a strictly positive gap at every finite horizon, and each of the two
known plateau weights has an exact equilibrium at which the debt vanishes once
that pin is released.  A positive plateau is therefore evidence that the
zero-boundary chain grammar has missed the equilibrium, not that none exists.

One carrier that replaces the pinned chain is a \emph{cycle}: finitely many rows
together with values that the rows reproduce, complementary at every phase, and
absorbing in the sense that the joint continuation mass over one period is below
one.  The mechanism layer says precisely when several one-stage mechanisms work: the
sure-exit test for pure coalitions, the collision-repair conditions priced
against the exact \(\chi\), the instant-punishment criterion, and the audited
two-owner indifference cell.  It also says
what cannot substitute for them: occupation-scaled rare phases add nothing
pointwise, and one blocker never suffices where two are needed.

At three coordinates the open question is therefore no longer a search over an
unbounded grammar; it is one finite semialgebraic decision.  Either the residue
after the mechanism layer is empty --- a coverage theorem closing the local
case --- or a member survives and must be adjudicated against the exact-cycle
and circulation engines, whose data (continuation values, triple-cell
interactions) the mechanism scalars provably do not determine.  For the general
program, the universal truncated-ledger route is formally impossible: even a
two-player zero-sum game with an exact all-Continue uniform equilibrium need
not admit its common deleted-survival cap.  The viable frontier therefore
branches between persistent-live/value-sensitive tails and projective or cyclic
producers.  Closing one exhaustive finite-quitting producer would settle the
quitting conjecture; the general finite-stochastic-game conjecture would still
require a separate reduction or producer.
