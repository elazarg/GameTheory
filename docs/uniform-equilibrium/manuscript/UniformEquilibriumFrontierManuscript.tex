\begin{abstract}
This is a research atlas rather than a publication claiming a new general
existence theorem.  It is written for a mathematically capable project owner,
adjacent researchers, and future proof agents.  It separates production Lean,
checked experiments, audited mathematics, conditional statements, open
problems, and refuted proof patterns.

The central open statement is that every finite stochastic game has a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  The repository has a semantic verification waist,
discounted and analytic source machinery, public-response composition,
several full special cases, and a detailed finite-quitting reduction.  At
HEAD \texttt{e1fe7dc}, finite calibrated quitting-chain blocks have exact
associative multi-player boundary holonomy.  The live P0 gap is deliberately
narrower: prove closedness (or a sound replacement) for realized,
anchor-preserving holonomies of unbounded middle length, then decode a seam or
exit as an executable repair or a uniform optimized-debt descent.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Uniform equilibrium, with its quantifiers visible}

Let \(I\) be a finite player set, \(S\) a finite state space, and
\(A_i(s)\) finite nonempty action sets.  A finite stochastic game has bounded
stage rewards \(g_i(s,a)\) and transition law \(q(\,\cdot\mid s,a)\).
Histories, states, and realized action profiles are public; players have
perfect recall and private independent randomization.  The base model grants
no free public correlation device.

For a behavior profile \(\sigma\), write
\[
 \gamma_N^i(s,\sigma)
 =\EE_{s,\sigma}\left[\frac1N\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}g_i(s_t,a_t)\right].
\]
A vector \(v\in\RR^I\) is a uniform-equilibrium payoff from \(s\) when
\[
 \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\sigma\ \exists N_0\
 \forall N\geq N_0\ \forall i\in I\ \forall\alpha_i,\qquad
 |\gamma_N^i(s,\sigma)-v_i|\leq\varepsilon,\qquad
 \gamma_N^i(s,\alpha_i,\sigma_{-i})\leq v_i+\varepsilon .
\tag{UE}
\]
The profile may depend on the accuracy, but not on the eventual horizon.
The deviator may be a history-dependent behavior strategy.  Thus a
horizon-by-horizon Nash profile, a limiting-average payoff, or a discounted
equilibrium does not by itself solve (UE).

\subsection{Program map}

\[
\boxed{\begin{array}{c}
\text{finite game}\\
\downarrow\quad\text{discounted or finite-chain source data}\\
\text{implementable full payoff target and legal continuation}\\
\downarrow\quad\text{delivery, all unilateral caps, and rebasing}\\
\text{adaptive certificate / deviation-cap constructor}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{uniform-equilibrium payoff.}
\end{array}}
\tag{1}
\]

The final verification arrow is production Lean.  The generic difficulty is
the middle.  A construction must retain the whole target vector, make every
public entry credible, and exhibit a well-founded decrease or a fully
discharged account.  A scalar limit or an attractive Bellman equality cannot
carry those obligations automatically.

An undiscounted equilibrium can be a limit along changing approximate
profiles, while a uniform payoff requires one profile for all sufficiently
large horizons at each accuracy.  A conversion therefore needs a named
argument.  Positive recursive games obtain one from monotonic finite
averages; finite quitting games use a different, formalized
terminal-to-uniform bridge.

\section{Production foundation: the two waists}

\subsection{Semantic cap constructor}

\Status{\Prod} The theorem
\texttt{hasUniformDeviationCapConstructor\_iff} says that a quantitative
delivery-and-deviation-cap constructor is exactly (UE).  A future proof may
use another construction language, but it must ultimately expose those
quantifiers.

\subsection{Adaptive potential verification}

\Status{\Prod} \texttt{AdaptiveCertificate.lean} verifies expectation-level
adaptive potential certificates.  At accuracy \(\delta\), a public profile is
equipped, playerwise, with lower and upper on-path potentials and a deviation
upper potential.  Their initial values are close to \(v\); on-path processes
have the right expected sub/supermartingale directions; and the deviation
bound applies to every unilateral behavior.

If \(L_i,U_i,D_i\) denote the potentials, the proof has the telescoping
shape
\[
\EE\sum_{t<N}(g_i(t)-v_i)
\quad\hbox{between}\quad
\EE[L_i(N)-L_i(0)]-o(N)
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\EE[U_i(N)-U_i(0)]+o(N),
\tag{2}
\]
with the same upper estimate using \(D_i\) after a unilateral replacement.
Division by \(N\) gives (UE), uniformly in the deviator.  Branch cancellation
is allowed; a construction need not have a pathwise nonnegative account, but
it must compile to this expectation-level control or directly to the semantic
cap constructor.

\subsection{Supplied public response architectures}

\Status{\Prod} A separate verification route handles a supplied finite public
Markov response architecture.  It separates the target and occupation data:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{(T0)} & v=P^0v\quad\text{on the prescribed delivery domain},\\
\text{(Ti)} & v_i\geq P_i^a v_i\quad\text{on player \(i\)'s owner arena},\\
\text{(N)} & \text{every invariant owner-local occupation has nonpositive
reward charge},\\
\text{(P)} & \text{the prescribed delivery law matches the target}.
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
Gain--bias/Poisson inequalities convert these conditions to bounded
potentials, a common \(O(1/N)\) delivery bound, unilateral caps, and
rebasing.  The domain split is load-bearing: the prescribed delivery union is
not the union of every player's deviation arena.

The fixed-class converse is also now production Lean.  All-start history
semantics on these exact domains imply target, occupation, and bias packets.
This characterizes a supplied architecture; it does not synthesize an
architecture for an arbitrary game or show that finite public architectures
cover all uniform strategies.

\subsection{Composition and concrete controllers}

\Status{\Prod} Finite public trees have backward Nash and fixed-depth adaptive
splicing.  Public stopping modules handle bounded causal stopping, stopped
child laws, prefix accounting, and explicit amortization horizons.  They make
real child systems composable; they do not create a missing child or a free
public signal.

\Status{\Prod} The Flesch--Thuijsman--Vrieze three-player quitting example is
implemented as a ten-configuration controller with a three-phase live clock
and seven absorbing children.  It satisfies the credibility conditions and
delivers \((1,2,1)\).  Under the repository's convention the all-start
finite-horizon coordinate errors are
\[
 16/(7N),\qquad22/(7N),\qquad18/(7N).
\tag{4}
\]
The uniform constant is \(22/(7N)\); the smaller value sometimes quoted uses
a different convention for the quitting stage.  This is an exact test of the
verifier for supplied data, not a generic controller producer.

\section{Discounted analytic source data}

\subsection{The analytic germ and endpoint atlas}

For normalized discount \(\lambda\), a stationary discounted equilibrium has
\[
 V_i^\lambda(s)=\EE_{s,\sigma^\lambda}
 \left[\sum_{t\geq0}\lambda(1-\lambda)^t g_i(s_t,a_t)\right].
\tag{5}
\]
\Status{\Prod} \texttt{AnalyticBellmanExistence.lean} proves that every finite
game has, after finite reparameterization, a coupled real-analytic Bellman
germ
\[
 t\longmapsto(\lambda(t),\sigma(t),V(t)),\qquad\lambda(t)\downarrow0.
\tag{6}
\]
On a punctured interval the finite families of supports, equality patterns,
and signs stabilize.  \texttt{AnalyticEndpointAtlas.lean} and
\texttt{AnalyticEndpointExistence.lean} then classify a first unprocessed
response into a finite typed leaf family carrying player, support, analytic
order, and occupation information.

This is source organization, not strategic closure.  A nonsemantic leaf may
ask for a response, target transport, entry proof, or credibility witness.
Such a field must remain an explicit hypothesis; it is not a generic proof
hidden inside an interface.

\subsection{Endpoint is not target}

\Status{\Prod} Sorin's absorbing example permanently blocks the most tempting
shortcut.  The relevant discounted equilibrium payoff is
\[
 z^\lambda=(1/2,\,2/3),
\]
but the repository proves that this endpoint is not a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  The proof combines security, live-tail
accounting, and occupation vanishing.  The converse characterization of the
whole uniform segment from Sorin's paper is not formalized \cite{Sorin1986}.
Thus
\[
 \text{discounted analytic source}\ \not\Rightarrow\
 \text{preserve its endpoint as a uniform root target}.
\tag{7}
\]
One may need both favorable-germ selection and a different, implementable
target selection.

\subsection{Major landed analytic infrastructure}

\Status{\Prod} The production graph also contains sign-stable Bellman rows,
neutral-action drift alternatives, harmonic directions, support-face and
deflation tools, player-owned and neutral occupation/circulation alternatives,
public separator compression, causal stopping and target-transport
interfaces, and rank-aware public recurrent-class machinery.
\Status{\Audit} The associated durable mathematics includes sharp neutral
drift, moving-bias minimax, saturated Puiseux rank, multiscale Markov
occupation, and the distinction between ordinary and causal transport.

The repeated obstacle is whole-target custody.  A scalar circulation or
positive charge might help one player, but it does not specify where the
remaining coordinates go after a public branch or why every player can
credibly continue there.

\section{Finite quitting games: exhaustive reduction to one residual}

\subsection{Model and terminal semantics}

A finite quitting game has one live state.  At every live date each player
chooses Continue or Quit; the first nonempty quitter set \(J\subseteq I\)
absorbs with reward vector \(r(J)\), while Never has payoff zero.  For a
time-dependent product profile \(x=(x^t)_t\) and first absorption time
\(\theta\),
\[
 u_i(x)=\EE_x\bigl[\one_{\{\theta<\infty\}}r_i(J_\theta)\bigr].
\tag{8}
\]
\Status{\Prod} The quitting semantics identify a fixed profile's limiting
finite-average payoff with (8).  Against fixed opponents, arbitrary
behavioral deviation has pure deterministic quit-time and Never extremals,
with a general stopping-law formulation.  Thus terminal cap calculations are
all-behavior calculations, not only stationary best replies.

\subsection{The final consumer is already available}

\[
 [\,\forall\varepsilon>0,\ \exists\hbox{ terminal }
 \varepsilon\hbox{-Nash profile}\,]
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \exists v\ \hbox{a uniform-equilibrium payoff}.
\tag{9}
\]
\Status{\Prod} \texttt{QuittingTerminalUniformization.lean} supplies the
finite-horizon bridge, and
\texttt{QuittingTerminalUniformPayoffSelection.lean} uses compact payoff
selection to retain one target \(v\).  The active problem is therefore the
producer of terminal approximate profiles for the general multi-player game.

\subsection{Finite exact chains and dynamic debt}

For each cutoff, production modules build compact sets of exact
zero-boundary Nash--Bellman chains.  Backward recursion compares prescribed
value with best stopping value; the difference is playerwise dynamic debt
\(B_i^t\).  The optimized aggregate initial debt \(d_K\) is attained and
\[
 d_{K+1}\leq d_K,\qquad d_K\geq0.
\tag{10}
\]
The chain factory, minimizer, calibration, monotonicity, and exact
chain-plus-Never semantics are \Status{\Prod}.  Hence the first split is
exhaustive:
\[
 \lim_{K\to\infty}d_K=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{terminal approximate equilibria at every accuracy}.
\tag{11}
\]
The zero-debt branch closes through (9); only a positive plateau
\(d_K\downarrow d_\infty>0\) remains.

\subsection{What positive debt forces}

\Status{\Prod} Calibration selects an owner \(i_*\) with positive solo debt
on minimizing chains.  Its exact-D tail has summable opponent-absorption
clock.  Marginal-hazard domination then makes the owner clock split exhaustive:
\[
 \sum_t h_{i_*}(t)=\infty\qquad\hbox{or}\qquad
 \sum_t h_{i_*}(t)<\infty.
\tag{12}
\]
In the first case, exact Bellman/Nash identities close by an exact terminal
equilibrium.  In the second, every individual hazard and every player's
opponent clock is summable.  There is no residual \(2^{|I|}\) clock lattice.

Positive debt also yields a marked simultaneous packet: nonempty
\(T_*\), positive owner joining loss, and raw terminal mass bounded below by
debt.  The required datum separates
\[
 \underbrace{\prod_{t=0}^{K-2}c_{i_*}(x^t)}
 _{\text{preterminal opponent survival}}
\quad\hbox{from}\quad
 \underbrace{p_{-i_*}^{K-1}(T_*)}_{\text{final marked atom}}.
\tag{13}
\]
The survival product cannot include the final root, whose opponent-continue
mass may be zero.  This is a proved correction to a former single-scale
compactification shortcut.

\subsection{The fully summable residual}

The P0 task is now exact: from a fully summable positive plateau carrying the
complete marked packet, produce an executable accuracy-indexed repair, or a
bounded exact extension that lowers optimized initial debt by a
cutoff-independent positive amount.  This is QG4 and PB7--PB8 in the
lifecycle records, and it is \Status{\Open}.  Its failure would refute this
particular producer only after a genuine falsifier; another stationary,
cyclic, or behavioral repair could still exist.

\section{Boundary holonomy after HEAD \texttt{e1fe7dc}}

\subsection{The landed finite middle}

\Status{\Prod} \texttt{QuittingBoundaryHolonomy.lean} packages every nonempty
finite block from one actual common product-root chain.  For each player, it
records prescribed and unilateral actions on a terminal continuation value:
\[
 G(w)=B+Pw,\qquad F(w)=\max\{A,T+\chi w\},\qquad0\leq P,\chi\leq1.
\tag{14}
\]
With relative terminal allowance \(\beta\), exact block regret is
\[
 F(w+\beta)-G(w).
\tag{15}
\]
The all-behavior cap is exactly the two affine inequalities
\[
 A-B-Pw\leq\varepsilon,\qquad
 T-B+(\chi-P)w+\chi\beta\leq\varepsilon.
\tag{16}
\]
The Lean theorems identify this evaluation with the literal finite prescribed
hazard value and finite Bellman best-response stopping value.

Chronological composition is associative:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
(B,P)\circ(B',P')=(B+PB',PP'),\\
(A,T,\chi)\circ(A',T',\chi')=
(\max\{A,T+\chi A'\},T+\chi T',\chi\chi').
\end{array}
\tag{17}
\]

\subsection{Anchoring is essential data}

The calibrated wrapper retains all common roots, exact-D entry and exit,
minimizer provenance, owner \(i_*\), full marked action/set \(T_*\), and the
two packet factors in (13).  Scalar coordinates lie in a common compact box.
This preserves cross-player compatibility that independent scalar summaries
would lose, and it makes seam acceptance a concrete finite test.

\subsection{What did not land}

Compactness of the scalar box is not closedness of the subset realized by
arbitrary-length anchored blocks.  Projection forgets roots, support changes,
chronology, packet, and calibrated prefix.  A convergent coefficient sequence
need not be a realized finite block with legal re-entry at the original root.

\Status{\Exp} The two-ended compactification probe extracts a forward positive
exact-D ray and a reverse ray ending on a zero-payoff/singleton-cap face, with
a positive reverse-depth-one packet.  The finite middle escapes.  It does not
give a bi-infinite orbit, transport a preselected atom across the middle, or
construct a repair.

The P0 gap after \texttt{e1fe7dc} is therefore
\[
\begin{array}{c}
\text{realized calibrated finite blocks}\\
\Downarrow\ ?\quad\text{closed or soundly relaxed anchored correspondence}\\
\text{close seam or certified buffered exit}\\
\Downarrow\ ?\quad\text{repair or uniform root-debt descent}.
\end{array}
\tag{18}
\]
Both arrows are \Status{\Open}.

\subsection{Why descent must be uniform}

If at fixed accuracy there are \(L,c>0\), independent of large cutoff, such
that every calibrated chain repairs or has
\[
 d_{K+L}\leq d_K-c,
\tag{19}
\]
iteration contradicts \(d_K\downarrow d_\infty>0\).
\Status{\Audit} A decrement \(c_K>0\) with \(c_K\to0\) is compatible with a
plateau.  The needed decoder must report a debt drop measured at the original
entry, not a local later improvement.

\subsection{Positive routes outside the quitting producer}

\paragraph{Analytic leaf closure or arc improvement.}
The endpoint atlas can be attacked leaf by leaf, or a theorem can select a
more useful analytic germ.  Either approach still must select an implementable
root target and compile legal public continuations.  Sorin prevents treating
germ selection alone as sufficient.

\paragraph{Fixed architecture synthesis.}
The gain--bias characterization in (3) is a clear synthesis target.  A
positive theorem would produce a finite controller, declare its delivery union
and owner arenas, prove target transport and neutral occupation, then invoke
the production verifier.  Open questions are why a suitable finite
architecture exists, how large it must be, and whether general strategies
require hidden or unbounded memory.

\paragraph{Vanishing-discount response synthesis.}
\Status{\Open} A controlled analytic family of discounted
APS/self-generating certificates might have a gain--bias limit
\[
 V^\lambda\approx g+\lambda^{-1}h,\qquad g+h=r+Ph.
\tag{20}
\]
The consumer exists in production Lean.  Pointwise value convergence does not
supply bounded bias: changing supports, multichain classes, and singular
resolvent scales can invalidate one fixed architecture.  A positive result
needs stable domains/support or a sound resolved limit; a chattering example
would delimit this route only.

\paragraph{Endogenous correlation.}
The correlated-existence theorem points to a possible mechanism but also a
boundary.  An autonomous device uses private current recommendations and
delayed disclosure; it is not merely a public random bit.  Public-coin and
correlation-separator modules test legal pieces of this question, including a
target-specific absorbing lift that independent randomization cannot realize.
A compiler must reproduce the needed information and obedience structure with
legal play and sublinear cost.

\subsection{What a negative route would have to prove}

A genuine counterexample needs a finite game and \(\eta>0\) such that every
behavior profile has a player and unilateral behavior deviation with the
required positive gain at the relevant long horizon.  Failures of stationary,
periodic, finite-memory, owner-solo, or selected-chain grammars do not meet
this burden.

For an actual stationary product profile \(x\), let \(u_i(x)\) be terminal
payoff and \(c_i(x)\) its exact full-rate behavior cap.  The honest stationary
filter is
\[
 g_{\mathrm{stat}}(x)=\max_i\max\{0,c_i(x)-u_i(x)\},\qquad
 \delta_{\mathrm{stat}}=\inf_x g_{\mathrm{stat}}(x).
\tag{21}
\]
\Status{\Audit} If \(\delta_{\mathrm{stat}}=0\), actual stationary terminal
approximate equilibria exist at every accuracy; if it is positive, only the
stationary class is excluded.  A graph-closure zero is an escape family, not
an attained profile.

The two-player table
\[
 r(\{1\})=(1,-1),\quad r(\{2\})=(-1,-1),\quad r(\{1,2\})=(-2,0)
\tag{22}
\]
has stationary exploitability tending to zero but no exact behavioral terminal
Nash equilibrium.  It refutes closed-attainable-tail and compact-zero-implies-
root arguments, not finite-quitting existence.

\subsection{Ordered research agenda}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Test realized anchored-holonomy closedness.}  Retain roots,
exact-D entry/exit, \(i_*,T_*\), both packet factors, and splice legality.
Either prove a closed lift for unbounded middle length or exhibit calibrated
families with identical scalar limits but incompatible provenance.
\item \textbf{State a decoder before proving topology.}  A close seam must
produce an actual cap-certified repair; a buffered exit must establish (19)
at the original root.  It must declare target, tail/profile, unilateral caps,
entry law, and rank/account.
\item \textbf{Finish or delimit the fully summable producer.}  Permitted
outputs are a finite/static/cyclic repair, a calibrated prefix plus actual
tail, a repaired standard-proper path, or uniform debt descent.  Test every
claim against nonattainment and static-grammar regressions.
\item \textbf{Package the known two-player base case.}  Formalize the
six-scalar pure/no-pure split, vanishing owner-solo branch, role reversal, and
terminal-to-uniform consumer; retain exact nonattainment as a regression.
\item \textbf{Use the architecture characterization.}  Any public response
claim should declare the split delivery/owner domains and prove all of (3),
rather than replace behavior caps by one-step tests.
\item \textbf{Keep filters distinct from counterexamples.}  Stationary and
grammar gaps are valuable search certificates but not a refutation without
an all-behavior gap.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Acceptance test and conclusion}

Before accepting a generic closure, ask:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Which source branch is selected, and why is the complete root target
implementable rather than merely a limit?
\item What actual behavior profile or public architecture exists at each
accuracy?
\item Why are all unilateral history-dependent deviations capped?
\item Where is every target coordinate transported through public branching
and stopping?
\item Why is entry/rebasing legal after every invoked public history?
\item What decreases well-foundedly, or which realized account is discharged?
Is that decrease uniform enough to contradict a plateau?
\item Which existing semantic verifier consumes the object without a circular
field that asks for uniform equilibrium again?
\item Does it survive the permanent regressions: Sorin target mismatch, Big
Match adaptivity, split-domain failure, behavioral nonattainment,
owner-transfer failure, and static sure-set incompleteness?
\end{enumerate}

The repository is no longer pursuing a vague claim that discounted equilibria
should converge.  It has a checked semantic waist and several independently
useful theorem families.  The finite-quitting route has an exhaustive
zero-versus-positive-debt split, an exhaustive owner-clock split, and now
exact finite middle holonomy.  The hard remaining obligation is also
well-identified: retain realized strategic provenance across an unbounded
middle, then make the resulting seam or exit strategically executable.  That
is a concrete research target, not a reason to overstate the current proof.
