# Impact survey: restore general discrete PMF semantics

This is a source survey and migration design, not an implementation or a new architecture decision. The requested breadth is an arbitrary discrete `PMF` on an arbitrary carrier, including countably infinite support. It does not imply a law on infinite paths or a general measurable game. The proposed direction is to replace the canonical law used by game semantics with `PMF`, retain the signature/information split, and keep finite support as an explicit property where a proof or algorithm actually uses it. D2 and the D11 baseline exclusion would need new measured decisions; D3's refusal of a generic probability-monad class and D12's one-way dependency roots can remain.

## What is frozen today

`FinDist α` is definitionally `{ μ : PMF α // μ.support.Finite }` ([source](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/FinDist.lean#L31)). Its `bind` proves the union of supports finite ([source](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/FinDist.lean#L73)); `pi` similarly proves finite product support ([source](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/FinDist.lean#L1626)). Thus the restriction is on each *law*, even where the carrier is infinite. This is D2's adopted choice ([decision](decisions/D2-finite-law-representation.md)); D2 explicitly calls finite support a law capability, not a carrier assumption. It is also the reason the current real `expect` is unconditional: its sum has finite support. The RFC records D2, D3, D11, and D12 at [lines 134–144](GameTheory2Design.md#L134), discusses a countable PMF extension at [line 462](GameTheory2Design.md#L462), and reserves genuine path probability for a distinct measurable layer at [line 1399](GameTheory2Design.md#L1399).

The first semantic choke point is `GameForm.play : Profile sig → FinDist sig.Outcome` ([Form](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Form.lean#L28)); `GameForm.outcomeLaw` then binds a `FinDist (Profile sig)` through play ([line 55](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Form.lean#L55)). This affects stochastic outcome laws, mixed profiles (`GameSignature.mixed` and `GameForm.mixed`, [lines 134–142](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Form.lean#L134)), and CE/CCE joint laws, which are specializations of `IsEquilibrium` ([Equilibrium](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Equilibrium.lean#L187)). `WeakPreference` ranks `FinDist Outcome` directly ([Preference](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Preference.lean#L28)); `DeviationScheme.actLocal` and `IsEquilibrium.statusQuo` are also finite laws ([Deviation](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Deviation.lean#L35), [line 121](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Deviation.lean#L121)). `expectedUtility` accepts any `FinDist Outcome` and calls unconditional real expectation ([Utility](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Utility.lean#L44)). These are semantic restrictions, not merely proof conveniences. The carrier-only `GameSignature` and `Profile` definitions have no probability choice ([Signature](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Signature.lean#L29)).

The second choke point is protocol execution. `ExecutionProtocol.step` returns `FinDist State` ([Execution](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Execution.lean#L47)); chance laws and bounded runs inherit it ([lines 164–180](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Execution.lean#L164)). History runs, randomized choosers, and behavioral policies use finite laws ([History](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/History.lean#L182), [Randomized](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Randomized.lean#L34), [Information](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Information.lean#L694)). `InformationModel` itself owns signals and policies; changing the probability carrier need not merge that information structure into `ExecutionProtocol`. Belief and assessment data are further choke points: `BehavioralAssessment.belief` ([BehavioralAssessment](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/BehavioralAssessment.lean#L104)), `Assessment.Context.outcome` and `ofBelief` ([Assessment](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Assessment.lean#L46), [line 116](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Assessment.lean#L116)), and continuation play ([Continuation](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Continuation.lean#L38)). Proofs using positive support and conditioning need a new PMF-support formulation, especially the finite-support equivalence and mixed-policy conditioning in [Information](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Information.lean#L1544).

Other semantic data repeat the same policy: `BayesianGame.prior` ([Core/Bayesian](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Bayesian.lean#L26)), stochastic transition ([Stochastic/Basic](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Stochastic/Basic.lean#L19)), public monitoring signal law ([Repeated/Monitoring](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Repeated/Monitoring.lean#L36)), MAID owner-policy and chance-node law ([Languages/MAID/Basic](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Languages/MAID/Basic.lean#L69)), and Bayesian mechanism prior parameters ([Languages/BayesianMechanism](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Languages/BayesianMechanism.lean#L60)). NFG is a deterministic syntax and compiles with `FinDist.pure` ([Languages/NFG](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Languages/NFG.lean#L25)); EFG/FOSG compile through Protocol ([Languages/EFG](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Languages/EFG.lean#L30), [Languages/FOSG](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Languages/FOSG.lean#L25)). Their finite syntax or finite horizon does not itself justify a finite-support law requirement.

## Finiteness that should survive

Finite *index* sets remain necessary for simultaneous independent player products (`FinDist.pi` in [Form](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/Form.lean#L140) and [Information](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/Information.lean#L731)); the analogous PMF product should keep `[Fintype ι]` on that operation. It should permit infinite support in each coordinate. Finite horizons in Protocol, FOSG, repeated prefixes, and stochastic finite-horizon results remain horizons; a finite number of steps can still produce a countably supported law. The existing infinite repeated game in [Repeated/Basic](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Repeated/Basic.lean#L53) is deterministic path semantics and need not acquire a path law.

Finite-carrier simplex equivalences and compactness are theorem-specific. [Math/Probability/Simplex](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/Simplex.lean#L52), [Analysis/Simplex](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Analysis/Simplex.lean#L31), and [Analysis/Nash](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Analysis/Nash.lean#L72) legitimately use finite-dimensional coordinates. Their PMF specialization can use `[Fintype α]` or a finite-support certificate; an unrestricted PMF space is not a finite simplex, and the present Nash proof does not automatically establish existence for infinite strategy carriers. The executable verifier in [Finite/Algorithm](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Finite/Algorithm.lean) needs explicit enumerations and computable real weights, with [Finite/Correctness](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Finite/Correctness.lean) connecting those results to PMF semantics. A finite-support subtype or named `support.Finite` hypothesis should remain available for support sums, enumeration, finite transcript sampling, and exact finite conditioning, but no longer be required by `GameForm`, `WeakPreference`, or `ExecutionProtocol` data.

Unconditional real utility is the chief mathematical issue. General PMFs can assign divergent expectation to an unbounded real payoff. A direct replacement of `FinDist.expect` by an unrestricted `PMF → (α → ℝ) → ℝ` would silently change meaning or totalize divergence. Prefer an operation-local boundedness or integrability hypothesis for real expected utility; the generic preference relation on PMF needs no such assumption. The existing bounded PMF integral theorem in [CountablePMFExpectation](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Experimental/PostArchitecture/CountablePMFExpectation.lean#L28) is a representative acceptance proof. Its bridge to finite expectation is at [line 37](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Experimental/PostArchitecture/CountablePMFExpectation.lean#L37). Bayesian interim equations ([BayesianEquilibrium](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Core/BayesianEquilibrium.lean#L35)) and monitoring continuation payoff equations should be reviewed under the same rule. This should be decided before a mechanical replacement of `FinDist` tokens.

## Measured authored surface

The reproducible counts in this section are a snapshot of baseline commit `1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5`, before the EXP-122 PMF restoration probe was added. Run the recipe below from a checkout at that commit; counts on later revisions will change. It uses `Path('GameTheory').rglob('*.lean')`, UTF-8 text, and the literal `FinDist` token as a *substring*. The baseline has 500 authored Lean files; 335 contain it. Counts by first directory are: Core 32/46, Protocol 17/20, Languages 18/29, Math 11/21, Finite 1/2, Analysis 52/59, Repeated 10/14, Stochastic 4/8, Mechanism 11/35, Experimental 96/130, Tests 71/96, and other roots 12/40. This measures source review, not required edits. `FinDist` also appears in finite facts that should remain specialized.

For authored-import fanout at that same baseline commit, parse every `import GameTheory...` line in those files, resolve to local module names, and take graph reachability. `GameTheory.Math.Probability.FinDist` has 29 direct importers and 424 transitive authored importers; `Core.Form` 8/319; `Core.Preference` 4/288; `Core.Deviation` 2/285; `Protocol.Execution` 8/192; `Protocol.Information` 12/174; and `Math.Probability.Measure` 2/9. These are import-graph counts, not compile-time costs and not a claim that 424 files need theorem rewrites. The exact recipe is:

```python
from pathlib import Path
files = list(Path('GameTheory').rglob('*.lean'))
mods = {'.'.join(p.with_suffix('').parts): p for p in files}
imports = {m: set() for m in mods}
for m, p in mods.items():
    for line in p.read_text(encoding='utf-8').splitlines():
        if line.startswith('import '):
            imports[m].update(n for n in line[7:].split() if n in mods)
def reaches(start, target):
    seen, stack = set(), list(imports[start])
    while stack:
        x = stack.pop()
        if x not in seen:
            seen.add(x)
            stack.extend(imports[x] - seen)
    return target in seen
for target in ('GameTheory.Math.Probability.FinDist', 'GameTheory.Core.Form',
               'GameTheory.Protocol.Execution', 'GameTheory.Protocol.Information'):
    print(target, sum(target in xs for xs in imports.values()),
          sum(reaches(m, target) for m in mods if m != target))
```

## Existing escape routes and audit consequences

The representation owner already exposes `FinDist.toPMF` ([FinDist](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/FinDist.lean#L39)). [Math/Probability/Measure](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/Measure.lean#L23) converts finite laws to measures, proves map/bind/product compatibility, and converts probability measures back only on finite carriers ([line 68](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Math/Probability/Measure.lean#L68)). [Protocol/PolicyMeasure](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Protocol/PolicyMeasure.lean#L40) constructs infinite product measures over total pure policies and relates their finite restrictions to `FinDist.pi`. The direct countable-PMF expectation experiment cited above and the opt-in [StochasticInfinitePlayMeasure](https://github.com/elazarg/GameTheory/blob/1dba33272108204204758c6eaa5ef8ce49a428c5/GameTheory/Experimental/PostArchitecture/StochasticInfinitePlayMeasure.lean#L182) show two different breadth demands: discrete countable expectation and measurable infinite paths. The latter should remain a separate gate. None of these currently makes a countably supported law a canonical `GameForm.play` or `ExecutionProtocol.step`.

At the surveyed baseline, [Phase 2 audit](../scripts/phase2-audit.ps1#L176) allowed `PMF`, `toPMF`, `ENNReal`, and `toReal` only in the two representation owners, with four named experimental exceptions; EXP-122 adds its single probe as a fifth exception. The audit still expects zero representation tokens outside owners and named exceptions. A PMF core must amend this check deliberately: permit `PMF` as a semantic type in designated Core, Protocol, Languages, Repeated, and Stochastic modules; continue to confine `ENNReal`/`toReal` conversion and raw PMF implementation internals to probability/expectation owners; remove or redefine the `toPMF` leakage metric when no conversion is needed. Retain the negative checks on `Fintype.ofFinite`, `open Classical`/`noncomputable` in executable modules, and the Analysis/fixed-point dependency split ([D12](decisions/D12-dependency-boundaries.md)). Phase 2 import probes that currently expect `PMF` reachable but `stdSimplex` and `Polynomial` unreachable from Core must be rerun and baseline updated only for genuine dependency changes. [Phase 3 audit](../scripts/phase3-audit.ps1#L294) names policy-measure probes and the rejected experimental infinite-path boundary; update the named probes if their declarations change, while keeping the positive policy-measure and negative path-boundary intent. Audit changes are part of the migration evidence, not a blanket suspension of boundary checks.

## Dependency-ordered migration waves

1. **Record the reopened decision and prove the probability slice.** Reserve an EXP entry, state the revised D2/D11 kill conditions, and test PMF pure/map/bind, finite indexed product, positive-event conditioning, support, bounded real expectation, and agreement with finite laws. The hostile witness is a countably supported law on `Nat` with unbounded support, plus a bounded nonconstant payoff. Acceptance consumer: the countable PMF expectation theorem and a `GameForm` with infinite-support outcome law. Keep one canonical PMF law, with finite-support proofs as optional certificates.
2. **Move Core without changing its semantic decomposition.** Change `GameForm.play`, `WeakPreference`, deviation and equilibrium laws, and `GameSignature.mixed` to PMF. Preserve `GameSignature` as the carrier layer and `IsNash`/CE/CCE as specializations of the same `IsEquilibrium`. Settle operation-local expected-utility hypotheses before moving `UtilityGame.preference`. Acceptance: a countably supported outcome game, mixed Nash statement, and CE/CCE with a countably supported joint law; finite binary and NFG witnesses still elaborate through the canonical definitions.
3. **Move Protocol execution, then information.** Change `step`, runs, randomized chooser, behavioral/mixed policies, and beliefs/context together in import order. Test a bounded run whose state/history law has infinite support, then conditional choice and posterior at a positive-probability information site. Retain information/signal structure and support-aware legality. Reprove the finite counterfactual-cover results as finite *site* statements, not finite-law assumptions. Check the PolicyMeasure bridge against new PMF finite restrictions.
4. **Move language compilers and native stochastic/repeated data.** NFG, EFG/FOSG, Bayesian, MAID, stochastic transitions, and public monitoring should compile through the PMF Core/Protocol definitions. Acceptance: a chance node with countable branching through EFG/FOSG to `GameForm`; Bayesian prior with countably many type profiles; MAID chance node and stochastic transition with countable support; a monitored finite prefix with countably many signals. Preserve finite horizon as a bound on time only.
5. **Rebuild specialized analysis and execution gates.** Reattach finite simplex, Nash existence, stochastic discounted existence, and the finite verifier only under their actual finite-carrier/certificate hypotheses. Revisit repeated/monitoring payoff sums with boundedness or integrability. Acceptance: existing finite Nash/executable tests, the general PMF witnesses from earlier waves, and unchanged one-way Analysis dependencies. Run narrow Lake targets at each wave and a full build and both audits at the phase gate; record status in the delivery ledger in the same commit as evidence.

This is an impact map, not a claim that direct textual substitution will compile. In particular, support finiteness currently underwrites real expectations, support enumeration, and several conditioning proofs; those must be replaced by explicit local assumptions and PMF theorems before the broad consumer pass.
